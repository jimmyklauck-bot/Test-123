# Louisiana Trust Companies and Bank Trust Departments

Compiled 2026-09-25. Scope: every trust company based in Louisiana, plus the Louisiana-headquartered banks that run a trust department, plus out-of-state firms with a staffed trust office in the state.

**How to read the confidence column.** The regulator lists (OFI Active Trust Companies, OFI Trust Company Directors, OCC Trust Banks by State, FDIC BankFind trust powers) and most bank websites were blocked from direct fetch by the network proxy. Everything here comes from search-result snippets. "Confirmed" means a regulator snippet or several independent sources agree. "Partial" means one solid source. "Lead" means a name or trust charter only; call before outreach.

**Bottom line.** Louisiana has only one active state-chartered trust company, and it is a single-family private trust company. The only open-market trust company headquartered in the state is Argent (Ruston), which holds its charter in Tennessee. The OFI Active Trust Companies page showed 1 entry (updated 2026-06-08); the Trust Company Directors page showed 9 entries (updated 2026-07-14), which are that one company's directors.

## Gaps and how to close them

1. Read the OFI Active Trust Companies and Trust Company Directors pages directly to confirm First Private Trust Corporation is the one entry and get the other eight directors.
2. Pull the OCC Trust Banks Active (6/30/2026) PDF to confirm no Louisiana national trust bank remains.
3. Filter FDIC BankFind for Louisiana institutions with trust powers exercised and fiduciary assets above zero. This settles every Partial and Lead bank above.
