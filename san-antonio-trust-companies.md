# San Antonio Metro Trust Companies and Trust Departments

Compiled 2026-09-25. Metro: Bexar, Comal, Guadalupe, Kendall, Medina, Wilson, Atascosa, Bandera counties.

**How to read the confidence column.** The Texas Department of Banking (DOB) site and dataset, OCC, FDIC and most company sites were blocked from direct fetch. Everything comes from search-result snippets, including snippets of DOB pages. "Confirmed" means a regulator snippet or several sources agree. "Partial" means one solid source. "Lead" means weak; call before outreach.

**Bottom line.** No active standalone trust company is headquartered in the San Antonio metro. The trust channel here is bank trust departments at Frost, Broadway, Jefferson and Texas Partners, plus staffed offices of Argent, JPMorgan, BofA and others.
