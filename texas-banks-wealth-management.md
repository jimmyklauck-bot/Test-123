## 1. VERIFICATION CEILING — READ THIS FIRST

EVERY CRD number and EVERY registration status in this workbook is SEARCH-INFERRED, NOT VERIFIED. Not one regulatory record was opened, in either research pass.

The network egress proxy returned HTTP 403 for api.brokercheck.finra.org, files.brokercheck.finra.org, adviserinfo.sec.gov and banks.data.fdic.gov. Direct page fetching was blocked for EVERY domain tested without exception, including bank websites and Wikipedia. Web search was the only working channel, and it returns result titles, URLs and snippets — not pages.

So: no BrokerCheck report was read, no Form ADV was opened, no FDIC institution record was pulled. CRD numbers here came from the numeric segment of an individual's own BrokerCheck or IAPD record URL as it appeared in search results. That is a good signal. It is not verification.

STATUS was assigned using the page-title heuristic: a result titled 'NAME - Broker at FIRM' or 'Investment Adviser at FIRM' indicates a current registration at that firm; 'previously registered' or no record at all indicates the opposite. A title that names the PERSON as well as the firm is materially stronger than one naming only the firm, and rows say which they rest on.

WHAT THE SECOND PASS PROVED ABOUT THIS CEILING: running the check changes answers. Five HilltopSecurities advisors carried as LIKELY STALE were all confirmed ACTIVE once the check was actually run, and one recorded CRD turned out to belong to nobody by that name. Treat every unchecked row here as genuinely unchecked, in both directions.

Every name should still be re-checked on BrokerCheck before outreach. Section 7 ranks which ones matter most.

## 2. HOW TO READ A BLANK

A blank or 'not found' means NOT FOUND. It NEVER means 'confirmed absent'. Three buckets are tracked separately and labelled on each bank tab:

   (a) PROGRAM CONFIRMED, NO NAMES FOUND — the wealth program exists and was verified; the roster was not retrieved. Examples: American National Bank of Texas (six advisors known to exist, none named), Texas Partners Bank, American National Bank & Trust Wichita Falls, IBC, Lubbock National.

   (b) NO WEALTH PROGRAM FOUND — searched, and nothing surfaced. Inwood National Bank, R Bank, Texas Security Bank, Central Bank Houston, and Pioneer Bank at lower confidence. There is now also NO First Financial trust office in Weatherford, which was corrected from an earlier draft that listed it as an office with no names.

   (c) COULD NOT VERIFY EITHER WAY — never researched, or researched to an unresolved end. See the 'STILL NOT RESEARCHED' tab.

ONE ENTRY IS STRONGER THAN 'NOT FOUND': BANKPLUS HAS NO TEXAS PRESENCE AT ALL. Its own current CRA branch listing shows 80 branches across exactly four states — Mississippi, Louisiana, Alabama and Florida — with Texas nowhere. That is a positive negative, and the recommendation is to drop it from the Texas dataset.

Phone numbers, emails and addresses were never reconstructed or guessed. Where a practice main line, department line or shared mailbox is given rather than a personal one, the row says so explicitly.

## 3. BROKER-DEALER IS NOT THE CLEARING FIRM

These are two separate columns everywhere in this workbook and they are never conflated.

LPL, Raymond James, Ameriprise, Hilltop, J.P. Morgan, Merrill and Wells Fargo all self-clear. So does Cetera Investment Services, which became the first broker-dealer in the bank/institutional space to self-clear in 1991 — though it ALSO uses BNY Pershing as third-party custodian for certain IRA types and NFS/Fidelity at enterprise level, which is exactly why it must be confirmed per bank.

Where a clearing firm could not be confirmed from a document, the field reads 'not confirmed'. It is NOT filled in by inference from the broker-dealer. Across the Texas community banks, almost no clearing relationships were confirmed — the banks simply do not disclose them. Do not let those fields be filled in later by assumption.

CONFIRMED clearing relationships: Frost to NFS; Texas Capital to Pershing; BOK Financial to Pershing; Truist to NFS; PNC to NFS; TRB Wealth Management to Schwab and Fidelity; First Horizon to LPL (self-clearing) since August 2025; Trustmark to Raymond James (self-clearing) since 2025.

*** BROKER-DEALER IS THE LEAST DURABLE FIELD IN THIS DATASET. *** Among the out-of-state banks alone, THREE of six changed broker-dealer inside eighteen months: First Horizon moved to LPL (Aug 2025), Trustmark moved to Raymond James (2025), and b1BANK appears to have moved to Raymond James (Jul 2026). Meanwhile Raymond James FID is systematically pulling Gulf South bank programs OFF LPL while First Horizon went the other direction. Verify the BD on every call rather than trusting the file.
