# Texas Banks — Wealth Management, Trust, Private Banking and Financial Advising

**Which banks have programs, and what platform each one runs on.**

Compiled 6 September 2026. Research channel: web search only. See the Verification Ceiling below — this is the single most important thing to read before using any row.

Companion files: `texas-bank-advisors-by-bank.md` (the advisor roster in prose) and `texas-bank-advisors.xlsx` (one tab per bank, plus a filterable Master List and a Platform & Clearing tab). All three are generated from one dataset, so a correction in one appears in all three.

## 1. VERIFICATION CEILING — READ THIS FIRST

EVERY CRD number and EVERY registration status in this workbook is SEARCH-INFERRED, NOT VERIFIED. Not one regulatory record was opened.

The network egress proxy in the research environment returned HTTP 403 for api.brokercheck.finra.org, files.brokercheck.finra.org, adviserinfo.sec.gov and banks.data.fdic.gov. Direct page fetching was blocked for EVERY domain tested without exception, including bank websites and Wikipedia. Web search was the only working channel, and it returns result titles, URLs and snippets — not pages.

Consequently: no BrokerCheck report was read, no Form ADV was opened, no FDIC institution record was pulled. CRD numbers here were taken from the numeric segment of an individual's own BrokerCheck or IAPD record URL as it appeared in search results. That is a good signal. It is not verification.

STATUS was assigned using the page-title heuristic: a result titled 'NAME - Broker at FIRM' or 'Investment Adviser at FIRM' indicates a current registration at that firm. 'Previously registered' or no record at all indicates the opposite.

A SECOND, SEPARATE LIMIT: the research session had a hard cap of 200 web searches, shared across all seven parallel researchers, and it was EXHAUSTED. Individual researchers completed between 14 and 36 searches against plans of 60 to 100. Coverage is therefore uneven by design, and the NOT RESEARCHED tab lists exactly what was never touched.

Every name in this workbook should be re-checked on BrokerCheck before outreach. The Follow-up section below ranks which ones matter most.

## 2. HOW TO READ A BLANK

A blank or 'not found' means NOT FOUND. It NEVER means 'confirmed absent'. Three distinct buckets are tracked separately and are labelled on each bank tab:

   (a) PROGRAM CONFIRMED, NO NAMES FOUND — the wealth program exists and was verified; the roster was not retrieved. Examples: Southside, Broadway, Jefferson, IBC, Lubbock National, Austin Bank.

   (b) NO WEALTH PROGRAM FOUND — searched, and nothing surfaced. Only TWO banks are in this bucket with real evidence behind them: Inwood National Bank and R Bank, plus Pioneer Bank at lower confidence. Even these are 'not found in the searches run', not proof.

   (c) COULD NOT VERIFY EITHER WAY — never researched. See the NOT RESEARCHED tab. Nine institutions named in the brief received zero searches.

Phone numbers, emails and addresses were never reconstructed or guessed. Where a practice main line or shared mailbox is given rather than a personal one, the row says so.

## 3. BROKER-DEALER IS NOT THE CLEARING FIRM

These are two separate columns everywhere in this workbook and they are never conflated.

LPL, Raymond James, Ameriprise, Hilltop, J.P. Morgan, Merrill and Wells Fargo all self-clear. Cetera lets the institution or advisor choose between its own self-clearing arm, BNY Pershing, and NFS/Fidelity — which is precisely why it must be confirmed per bank.

Where a clearing firm could not be confirmed from a document, the field reads 'not confirmed'. It is NOT filled in by inference from the broker-dealer. Across the Tier 3 community banks, ZERO clearing relationships were confirmed — no bank disclosed one. Do not let those fields be filled in later by assumption.

CONFIRMED clearing relationships in this dataset: Frost to NFS; Texas Capital to Pershing; BOK Financial to Pershing; Truist to NFS; PNC to NFS; TRB to Schwab and Fidelity; Comerica to AEIS since Nov 2023, having moved from Pershing.

## 4. BRAND vs REGISTERED ENTITY

Most Texas community-bank wealth names are MARKETING BRANDS sitting on somebody else's broker-dealer, and the bank publishes a disclosure saying so. 'AB Financial Services' is LPL. 'IBC Investment Services' is LPL. 'LSNB Investment Services' is LPL. 'Southside Investment Services' is Raymond James. 'City Bank Wealth Management' is Raymond James. 'ProsperityPrivate' is Raymond James.

A few are genuine registered entities: Frost Brokerage Services, TCBI Securities, BOK Financial Securities, Hilltop Securities, and TRB Wealth Management, LLC.

Two carry a SECOND brand layer and are flagged as such: VeraBank markets 'CFG Financial Group' over Cetera, and Jefferson Bank markets '1900 Wealth' whose registered status was never determined.
