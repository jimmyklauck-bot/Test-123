## Inclusion bar

Every team here clears one of two tests: **published AUM of $250M+**, or **a verifiable multi-advisor team** (named advisors, multiple offices, or a recognition list with a known asset floor). Solo producers are excluded. The channel-median AUM estimates from pass 1 are gone — they were guesses dressed as data.

**BrokerCheck has no AUM field and no headcount field**, so it cannot rank anything by size. What it contributed is verified branch addresses and CRDs. The ranking comes from press releases, transition announcements and firm disclosures.

**Confidence.** Every advisor website, LinkedIn page and BrokerCheck page was blocked from direct fetch by the network proxy. "Published" means a press release or firm disclosure stated the figure. "Verified team" means named advisors or multiple offices appeared in search snippets from the firm's own site or a press release. "Confirmed practice" means firm and location confirmed, roster not public. Confirm registration on BrokerCheck before outreach.

## Three things to know before dialing

**Commonwealth is the timing play.** LPL is absorbing it, assets move by Q4 2026, and 328-plus advisors have already left. FCA Corp (#9), Selah (#22) and Merkle (#23) are all Commonwealth. Advisors mid-repaper rebuild their model portfolios, which is the only moment a lineup is genuinely open.

**Ameriprise needs one qualifying question.** ETF discretion lives in the rep-as-portfolio-manager program (SPS Advisor), not the home-office managed programs. Ask which one they run on call one.

**Two Houston firms are excluded but worth knowing.** NEXT Financial Group is headquartered here with 500+ advisors and roughly $13B, but Atria bought it and LPL bought Atria, so it is LPL now. Avidian is a Houston independent rather than an IBD, but Presidio (#3) broke away from it in 2023 and the relationship map matters if you call both.
