## Verification status — read this first

The ask was teams *currently* registered with an approved BD, not a former or unlisted one. **I cannot certify that from this session.** FINRA BrokerCheck and SEC IAPD are both blocked by the network proxy, so no row here is a live registration check.

Evidence grades used below:

- **BrokerCheck** — an indexed FINRA record shows the named individual at this BD. 2 of 36 rows.
- **Firm disclosure** — the firm's own site carries the BD's required FINRA/SIPC disclosure line. Reliable when current; sites lag departures.
- **Announcement** — a dated transition or partner-firm press release named the BD. Accurate as of that date only.

Run every name through BrokerCheck before outreach. That is about a minute per advisor and it closes exactly the gap flagged here.
