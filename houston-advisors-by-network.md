## How the score works

Points, transparent and additive. Recognition: Forbes Best-In-State Wealth Advisors 2026 ranked #3 (+45) or listed (+30); Barron's Top 1200/1500 (+20); top-5 Ameriprise producer 20+ yrs (+30); Forbes 2026 Team Member (+15); team on Forbes BIS Teams (+12). Team book: on a team with published AUM ≥$1B (+25) or ≥$250M (+15). Role: principal / PWA / MD / CEO / founder (+14); branch manager / VP / wealth manager (+8); advisor (+3). Credentials: CFA / CIMA / CPWA / CPA (+10 each, max 2); CFP (+10); ChFC / CLU / CEPA / CDFA / AIF / PFS (+4 each, max 3); CRPC / APMA / AAMS / BFA / RICP / CLTC / NSSA / CEP (+2 each, max 4); MBA / MS (+3). Tenure: 30+ yrs (+10), 20-29 (+7), 10-19 (+4). Multi-office (+5). Unconfirmed city (−6).

Tier 1 = 44+, Tier 2 = 25-43, Tier 3 = under 25. **Tier 3 is a confirmed, in-market, correctly-affiliated advisor with little public signal** — a discovery list, not a quality judgment.

**What this is not:** rep-level AUM. It exists only in licensed databases. Firm-level AUM (e.g., Sunbelt's $2B across 67 reps) is deliberately not credited to individuals.

## Why this is 237 and not 300

Every name here has a confirmed Houston-metro city and a confirmed non-excluded BD. Reaching 300 would have meant adding names whose city or BD I could not confirm, which is the error you flagged twice. The remaining population sits behind the Osaic, Cetera and Kestra advisor directories, which are blocked from this environment; `pull_houston_advisors.py` in this repo pulls them from your machine. Ameriprise is over-represented (121 of 237) only because it is the one BD that publishes an indexed page per advisor.
