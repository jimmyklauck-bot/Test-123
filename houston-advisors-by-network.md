## How the score works

Points, transparent and additive. Recognition: Forbes Best-In-State Wealth Advisors 2026 ranked #3 (+45) or listed (+30); Barron's Top 1200/1500 (+20); top-5 Ameriprise producer 20+ yrs (+30); Forbes 2026 Team Member (+15); team on Forbes BIS Teams (+12). Team book: on a team with published AUM ≥$1B (+25) or ≥$250M (+15). Role: principal / PWA / MD / CEO / founder (+14); branch manager / VP / wealth manager (+8); advisor (+3). Credentials: CFA / CIMA / CPWA / CPA (+10 each, max 2); CFP (+10); ChFC / CLU / CEPA / CDFA / AIF / PFS (+4 each, max 3); CRPC / APMA / AAMS / BFA / RICP / CLTC / NSSA / CEP (+2 each, max 4); MBA / MS (+3). Tenure: 30+ yrs (+10), 20-29 (+7), 10-19 (+4). Multi-office (+5). Unconfirmed city (−6). **Registration check:** current firm confirmed on BrokerCheck or IAPD (+6); evidence they have left, marked STALE (−10); not surfaced (0).

Tier 1 = 44+, Tier 2 = 25-43, Tier 3 = under 25. Tier 3 is an in-market, correctly-affiliated advisor with little public signal: a discovery list, not a quality judgment.

## Fit (0–10), a separate question from Score

Score says how big and how visible. Fit says how likely they are to put an ETF in a model. +3 portfolio-construction signal (CFA, CIMA, CPWA, APMA, AAMS, or runs the investment process); +2 controls the model (principal, PWA, partner, founder, MD); +2 open-architecture BD (Sanctuary, NewEdge, Rockefeller, Sunbelt, Kestra, Cetera, Osaic, Independent Financial Group), +1 Ameriprise (ask SPS Advisor vs managed on call one); +2 registration verified; −3 insurance or annuity lean; −2 403(b)/TRS niche; −2 employee-RIA channel (Cetera's TRPG); −1 unconfirmed city.
