Compiled 2026-09-11. **Pass 1 of an expected 4-5.** Networks covered so far: Ameriprise, Osaic, Cetera (incl. Avantax), Kestra/Bluespring, Cambridge, Commonwealth, Sanctuary, NewEdge, Corebridge/VALIC. Excluded by instruction: LPL, Raymond James, Northwestern Mutual, Equitable.

For comparison, `louisiana-advisors-by-network.md` reached ~90 firms across 7 networks over five passes. This file is at 45 after one. The gap is passes, not method.

## Read this before using the list

**BrokerCheck cannot answer "large team or substantial AUM."** It has no AUM field and no headcount field, for any firm or any rep. It holds registrations, employment history, exams and disclosures. That is a property of the database, not of this environment — nobody can run that query against BrokerCheck, however much access they have. What BrokerCheck did give here: verified branch addresses and CRDs, which is why the address column is trustworthy even where the size column is empty.

**So size below is measured two ways, and neither is AUM except where noted:**

- **Named advisors** — how many advisors at the firm I could actually name from the firm's own site or a press release. This is the honest proxy for "large team" and it is a floor, never a ceiling. A firm showing one name may have eight.
- **Published AUM** — only where a press release, transition announcement or firm disclosure stated it. Eight entries have this. Everything else is blank, deliberately. No estimates in this file.

**Confidence column.** Every advisor website, LinkedIn page and BrokerCheck page was blocked from direct fetch by the network proxy, so no page was read in full. "Confirmed" means the address and network affiliation both appeared in a FINRA BrokerCheck-derived result. "Partial" means both appeared in search snippets from the firm's own site or a press release. "Lead" means one signal only. Confirm on BrokerCheck before outreach.

## Gaps and how to close them

**What passes 2-5 should do,** in the order that pays best:

1. **Name the advisors at the 13 Ameriprise practices and 4 Osaic/Cetera firms currently showing "Not captured."** This is the highest-value work: advisor count is the only team-size proxy available, and these are already-confirmed firms. Query shape that works: the firm name plus "team" or "our advisors", which surfaces the site's team page in snippets.
2. **Run the BD-disclosure query per network.** Searching the exact disclosure string (`"securities offered through [BD name]"` plus a Houston-area city) surfaces DBA firms that no directory lists. This is how the Louisiana file found most of its Cambridge and Osaic entries. Worth running against every suburb separately: Sugar Land, Katy, The Woodlands, Cypress, Pearland, Kingwood, Clear Lake, Bellaire, Memorial, Spring, Conroe, League City, Friendswood, Missouri City, Richmond.
3. **Work the Commonwealth departure lists.** Trade coverage names departing teams and their destinations. Houston names in those lists are pre-qualified prospects in transition.
4. **Networks not yet touched for Houston:** Arkadios, Park Avenue Securities (Guardian), Steward Partners, Concurrent, Geneos, Private Client Services, Kovack, Prospera, Founders Financial, Securian, Lincoln. Louisiana found real firms at Arkadios and Park Avenue; Houston is a bigger market and should have more.

**What no number of passes will produce, and where to get it instead:** rep-level AUM. It exists only in licensed databases. Ask Pacer national accounts whether they license Discovery Data, AdvizorPro or FINTRX — AdvizorPro alone maps 450,000 reps across 5,000 broker-dealers, and the query is: Houston MSA, exclude the four firms above, sort by rep AUM, take 150. Ten minutes with a seat.

Free partial substitute: the SEC publishes Form ADV Part 1 as quarterly CSVs back to 2001, including Schedule D branch offices. That yields every advisory firm with a Houston-area office plus **firm-level** AUM — not rep-level, but it would let this file be sorted rather than grouped.
