# Houston Metro Trust Companies and Trust Departments

Compiled 2026-09-25. Metro: Harris, Fort Bend, Montgomery, Brazoria, Galveston, Waller, Chambers, Liberty, Austin counties.

**How to read the confidence column.** The Texas Department of Banking (TDOB) site and dataset, OCC, FDIC and most company sites were blocked from direct fetch. Everything comes from search-result snippets, including snippets of TDOB entity pages and the OCC Trust Banks Active (7/31/2026) list. "Confirmed" means a regulator snippet or several sources agree. "Partial" means one solid source. "Lead" means weak; call before outreach.

**Market changes that affect this list (2026):**
- Stellar Bank merged into Prosperity Bank on 7/1/2026. Stellar branches keep their name until about March 2027.
- Cadence Bank merged into Huntington on 2/1/2026. Cadence's trust business is now Huntington's.
- Comerica merged into Fifth Third on 2/1/2026. Rebrand expected Q3 2026.
- Houston Bank & Trust, a new bank with a trust department, opened in May 2026.
