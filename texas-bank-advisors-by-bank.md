## Before you use a single name here

Every registration status and every CRD number below is **search-inferred, not verified**. No BrokerCheck report, Form ADV or FDIC record was opened, because the research environment blocked all four regulatory sources and, in fact, blocked direct page fetching for every domain tested. Status rests on the page-title heuristic: a search result titled "NAME - Broker at FIRM" indicates a current registration at that firm, while "previously registered" or no record at all indicates the opposite.

The four status values mean this:

- **ACTIVE** — a registration record or a current bank source places this person at this firm now.
- **UNVERIFIED** — named by a credible current source, but no independent status signal was obtainable. For trust officers and private bankers, who are frequently not securities-registered at all, this is the expected value and not a warning.
- **LIKELY STALE** — evidence points to departure or retirement, or the only source is three or more years old with no current registration record. Treat it as a retirement flag.
- **UNRESOLVED** — sources conflict, or a name collision could not be broken.

Where a person is marked stale purely because the check could not be run rather than because departure evidence was found, the entry says so explicitly. Those are the cheapest names to rehabilitate.
